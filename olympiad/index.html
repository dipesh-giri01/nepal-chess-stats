Nepal at the Chess Olympiad

A record of every over-the-board appearance since the federation's 2004 debut, open and women's sections, through the 46th Olympiad in Samarkand. The 2020 Olympiad was postponed; 2021 was the separate FIDE Online Chess Olympiad (Nepal won Division 4 Pool A, then finished 8th in Division 3) and is not in this OTB series.

How big was the field

Total teams and nations per edition, from Wikipedia (2004–2024) and FIDE for Samarkand 2026: 208 Open and 192 Women. The Olympiad has nearly doubled since Nepal's 2004 debut; Samarkand is the largest ever.

Nepal's rank, adjusted for field size

Raw rank alone undersells the growth in competition: finishing 108th in a 146-team field (2008) is not the same achievement as 108th in a 200-team field. This shows Nepal's finish as a percentile: 100% would mean winning the whole Olympiad, 0% would mean finishing last.

Starting seed vs final rank

Where Nepal was seeded going in (by rating) versus where it actually finished. Real seed numbers pulled from Chess-Results' SNo column. The dataset's own "list_rank" field turned out to just duplicate final rank, so this required going back to the source. Includes 2016 and 2018 Women (Baku, Batumi). 2004 and 2006 Open still omitted: early standings files don't cleanly separate seed from finish.
